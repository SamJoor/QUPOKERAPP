Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 August 2026

QU Poker Club ("the app") is run by the Quinnipiac University poker club for its members. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and what you can do about it. It covers the iOS and Android apps.

The short version. We collect the account details you type in and a record of your club activity so the app can show your points and attendance. We do not sell your data, we do not share it with advertisers, and there is no analytics or tracking SDK in the app. You can delete your account and its data from inside the app at any time.

What we collect

Details you give us when you sign up or edit your profile:

  • Email address — used to sign you in and to send account emails
  • Your name — shown to other members on leaderboards and member lists
  • Graduation year and major — optional, shown on your club profile
  • Student ID — optional, used by officers to match you to club records
  • Profile picture — either an avatar you pick from the ones built into the app, or a photo you choose from your photo library

Records created as you use the app:

  • Club points, and the history of how you earned or spent them
  • Event check-ins, including which event and when
  • Tournament registrations and results
  • Reward redemptions and their status
  • Games you play in the app, including hands and results

Camera and photo access

The app asks for camera access only when you open the QR scanner to check in to a club event. It reads the code on screen and nothing else. No image is stored or transmitted.

The app asks for photo library access only when you choose to set a profile picture, and only the image you pick is uploaded. You can use one of the built-in avatars instead and never grant photo access at all.

Both permissions are optional. Declining either leaves the rest of the app working normally.

What we do not collect

  • No location data
  • No contacts, calendar, or health data
  • No advertising identifiers, and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs
  • No payment details — the app takes no payments, and club points have no cash value

Where your data lives

Account details and club records are stored using Supabase, which hosts the app's database. Account emails — verification codes and password resets — are sent through Gmail's mail servers. Those providers process data on our behalf so the app can function.

Access is restricted by database security rules: members can read their own records, and club officers can see the club records they need to run events, points, and rewards.

Who can see your information

Other members can see your name, profile picture, graduation year, major, and your standing on club leaderboards. Club officers can additionally see your email, student ID, check-in history, and points history in order to administer the club.

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with anyone outside the club except the service providers named above.

Deleting your account

Open Profile in the app and choose Delete Account. This removes your profile, points, check-ins, redemptions, and game history. It cannot be undone, and points cannot be restored if you sign up again later.

Records that are part of club history rather than personal data — for example that an event took place — are kept, with your details removed from them.

If you cannot access the app, email us at the address below and we will delete your account for you.

Children

The app is intended for Quinnipiac University students and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update this page and change the date at the top.
