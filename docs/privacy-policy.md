# QU Poker Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 7, 2026

QU Poker is a student club app for poker strategy education, attendance, engagement points, rewards, friendly tournaments, and non-gambling competition.

## Data We Collect

- Account email for login.
- Full name for club membership and event participation.
- QU Student ID for officer membership verification.
- Optional major and graduation year for club context.
- Optional profile photo if the member chooses to upload one.
- Attendance, point ledger, tournament registration, table assignment, reward redemption, and practice activity records.

## How Data Is Used

Data is used to manage club membership, QR attendance, engagement points, non-cash rewards, tournament registration, table assignments, and educational poker practice.

## What We Do Not Do

- No real-money wagering.
- No deposits, withdrawals, cash-outs, casino wallet, or payment features.
- No tracking for advertising.
- No sale of student data.

## Student ID

Student ID is required only to help officers verify club membership eligibility. It should not be shown on public profiles or leaderboards.

## Camera and Photos

Camera access is used only to scan event QR codes. Photo library access is used only if a member chooses a profile photo.

## Contact

For support or privacy questions, contact the club officers or the app support contact listed in App Store Connect.
