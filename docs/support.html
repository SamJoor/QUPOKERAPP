Tell us your account email and what happened, and we'll sort it out.

I didn't get my verification code

Check your spam or junk folder first — mail from a new sender often lands there, and marking it "not spam" makes sure the next one reaches your inbox. Codes can take a minute to arrive.

If it still hasn't shown up, tap Resend code on the verification screen.

One thing worth knowing: if you already have an account with that email address, signing up again sends nothing at all. Use Forgot Password on the login screen instead.

I can't log in

You must verify your email before you can sign in. If you never entered the code, sign up again with the same address and you'll be taken back to the verification screen.

Forgotten your password? Use Forgot Password on the login screen and open the reset link on your phone.

My points look wrong

Points appear after an officer records them or after you check in to an event. Your full history is on your Profile screen — if something is missing from it, email us with the event and date and an officer will check the club records.

The QR scanner won't open

The app needs camera permission to scan event check-in codes. If you declined it, enable it in your phone's Settings under QU Poker. You can also ask an officer to check you in manually.

I want to delete my account

Open Profile and choose Delete Account. This permanently removes your profile, points, check-ins, and redemptions, and cannot be undone. If you can't get into the app, email us and we'll do it for you.

Something else is broken

Email us with your account email, your phone model, and what you were doing when it went wrong. A screenshot helps more than anything else.
