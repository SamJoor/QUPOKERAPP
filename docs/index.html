QU Poker Club

The club app — events, points, tournaments, and strategy practice

QU Poker Club is the app for members of the Quinnipiac University poker club. Check in to club events with a QR code, earn and spend club points, register for tournaments, follow the leaderboard, and play practice hands against other members.

Club points have no cash value. They cannot be bought, sold, or exchanged for money. No gambling or wagering takes place in this app — every game is play money for practice.
